🐸 Frogger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Frogger is a community discussion platform. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, and username.
  • Authentication data: OAuth tokens from third-party providers (GitHub, Google) used solely for sign-in.
  • Session data: browser session identifiers to keep you signed in.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To authenticate your identity when you sign in.
  • To display your profile within the community.

4. Third-Party Services

We use OAuth authentication via GitHub and Google. When you sign in through these providers, we receive limited profile data (name, email, avatar) as authorized by you. We do not access your private repositories, files, or contacts.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase. Sessions are encrypted and transmitted over HTTPS.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, all personal data will be permanently removed.

8. Cookies

We use essential session cookies to maintain your authenticated state. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our project repository or contact the developer.
